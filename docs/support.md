# Editify Support

**Last updated:** 22 September 2026

## What Editify is

Editify is a video editor with an assistant that drives the same timeline you do. You import clips, cut by transcript, caption to the word, add sounds, stickers and voiceover, then render up to 4K. The app is a client: editing, transcription and rendering happen on a server we run, so Editify needs an account and an internet connection.

Editify is published by Maja Ventures SL.

## Getting help

Write to {{FILL_CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will answer. It helps if you say:

- what you were trying to do, and what happened instead,
- which device and app version you are on (the version is printed at the bottom of the sign-in screen),
- the name of the project, if the problem is in one particular project.

You can also report a problem without leaving the app. The **send feedback** button on the home screen and in the editor sends us the error, a short description of the screen you were on, and whatever you type. If the app crashes, the same report is offered to you before you carry on.

## Subscriptions

Paid plans (such as Creator) are auto-renewing subscriptions sold through the App Store and Google Play. The price, the billing period and the length of any free trial are shown in the app before you buy.

- **Restoring a purchase.** Sign in with the same Editify account and press "restore purchases" on the subscription screen. Entitlements follow the account, not the phone.
- **Cancelling.** Cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.
- **Refunds.** Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We cannot issue a refund for a purchase made in a store.
- **Deleting your Editify account does not cancel a store subscription.** Cancel it in the store first.

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** on the Editify home screen erases your projects, the clips you imported, your renders, the diagnostic reports stored against your account, and your login. It cannot be undone.
